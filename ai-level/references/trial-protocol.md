# Trial Protocol

## Goal

Run a small trial with 3-5 colleagues to check whether the diagnostic feels short, fair, and useful.

## Trial Steps

1. Give the respondent the main scenario.
2. Let them answer freely. Do not coach them.
3. If they mention Agent, Skill, MCP, automation, reusable workflow, or tool chains, ask one adaptive anchor follow-up.
4. Produce the report.
5. Ask for feedback.

## Feedback Questions

Ask:

- Did the result feel basically accurate?
- Did any wording feel confusing or too abstract?
- Did the test feel like a real challenge rather than a questionnaire?
- Was the next-level advice useful?
- Did the confirmed level feel too harsh, too generous, or fair?

## Calibration Notes

Record:

- Estimated signal level.
- Confirmed level.
- Evidence strength.
- Whether the anchor follow-up changed the level.
- One wording change to improve the test.

## Pass Criteria

The first version is good enough to turn into a website or installed skill when:

- At least 3 of 5 respondents say the result feels explainable.
- No respondent reports that the scenario feels like a business assignment.
- The anchor follow-up catches at least one inflated advanced answer.
- The next-level advice produces a concrete action, not generic learning advice.
